# Changelog

[日本語](CHANGELOG.md)

## [v2026.10.26](https://github.com/nagataaaas/wots4-tweaks/releases/tag/v2026.10.26)

- Added Infinite Items. Food, drinks, whetting stones, Moves Scrolls, Medicine and other items the hero uses are not used up. Usable items handed over in missions, such as onigiri, are kept too.
- With Mixed-Stance My Style, the stance list now ends with Common: the parries, Toughen, the eating and drinking moves and Immovable Heart of every stance, and the stance's own Renki Koho. Spear, unarmed, pistol and rifle stances can use it too.
- With Mixed-Stance My Style, defence moves of different types can now be set across stances.

## [v2026.10.19](https://github.com/nagataaaas/wots4-tweaks/releases/tag/v2026.10.19)

- Rebuilt against ModLoader v2026.10.19. No changes to the features.

## [v2026.10.18](https://github.com/nagataaaas/wots4-tweaks/releases/tag/v2026.10.18)

- Added battle tweaks: Enemy Spawn Interval, More Enemies at Once, Enemy Pool Size, Dropped Item Limit and Defeated Enemy Cleanup.
- Added Weapon Durability. Choose from Default / No Loss / Break at Once.
- Added Big Shot in Move List. Moves whose power Big Shot raises get a BS or BS- mark.
- Options that take effect after a restart now show a notice below them when changed from their current state.
- With Mixed-Stance My Style, Iai moves taken into another stance no longer sheathe the sword at their end.

## [v2026.10.15](https://github.com/nagataaaas/wots4-tweaks/releases/tag/v2026.10.15)

- Renamed the ☆4 Challenger choice Masked Samurai to Masked Clean Samurai.
