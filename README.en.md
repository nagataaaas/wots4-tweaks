# tweaks

[日本語](README.md)

> [!NOTE]
> This Mod is for the [Steam version of Way of the Samurai 4](https://store.steampowered.com/app/312780/4/?l=english) only.

A collection of small "nice to have" adjustments (tweaks). Each tweak can be switched on its own from the "Tweaks" page added to the game's options.

## Features

- **Laura's Night Crawling**: fix who waits in the futon when you have a night crawling appointment with Laura.
  - Don't Fix: as in the game. Which one you get depends on things such as whether the game was restarted after the appointment.
  - Fake: when you lift the futon, someone disguised as Laura takes her place.
  - Real: the night crawling is with Laura herself.
- **Guaranteed Loot**: searching or kicking a body gives an item on the first try, when the body has something to give. Which item you get keeps the game's odds. Items that townsfolk drop on their own when they die are not changed.
- **Unlimited My Style**: when making My Style, you can add as many moves as you like even without enough Ability Points. The screen still shows "total / Ability Points", so the total may look larger than your points. Your Ability Points are not spent.
- **Instant Missions**: a mission succeeds as soon as you accept it and the conversation ends. The reward, the town's order and your records are the same as for a normal success (bonus rewards, such as for the number of enemies defeated, are not paid). Night missions accepted during the day succeed right away too.
  - The mission series of Nodobotoke, Kobato, the Thief, Gramps and Shirube, battle missions such as Underbelly Recon and Government Recon, and the last mission, The Amihama Eight, are included. Clients give their next request as after a normal success.
  - You do not see the fights and conversations with the targets. Accepting Grandpa's Memories (Part 1) or A Lid On Something Stinky (Part 1) also accepts Part 2, and both parts succeed.
- **Guaranteed Wooing**: when you woo someone, you always make a nighttime promise, whatever you answer. Wooing a named character (Akagi, Kogure, Kotobuki, Moro, Kinugawa, J.J. or Dojima) with Nightly Pleasure Martial Arts also makes the promise on the first try, at any level of the style. People who cannot be wooed, and anyone when you already have a partner for tonight, still turn you down.
- **Best Smithing**: when you ask the Smithy to Refine or Recast a weapon to make it stronger, the attack always goes up by the largest amount (Dojima's extra bonus too). Making it harder or polishing it always gives fixed values, so they are not changed.
- **Guaranteed Pupils**: asking someone to become your pupil always succeeds. Without a dojo, with the dojo closed, or with a full dojo (20 pupils), you are still turned down.
- **Pupils Survive Challengers**: dojo challengers no longer cost you pupils. Talking to the pupil who brings the news is an ordinary conversation, and no pupil dies or loses the dojo. Pupils who appear while challengers are in do not fall in the fight at the dojo, however hard they are hit. Pupils can still die in other ways, such as when you cut them down in town.
- **Pupils Stay in the Dojo**: your pupils no longer appear in town and stay in the dojo. When challengers come, no pupil comes into town to tell you. The number of pupils and the dojo's reputation are not changed.
- **Repelling Challengers**: choose whether the sensei and your pupils repel dojo challengers while they are in (decided each time you move to another area).
  - Default: as in the game.
  - Always: the sensei or your pupils always repel them.
  - Never: neither the sensei nor your pupils repel them.

  The options are shown in the game's language (Japanese or English).

## Installation

> [!WARNING]
> Install [wots4-mods (ModLoader)](https://github.com/nagataaaas/wots4-mods) first.

1. Close the game.
2. Download `wots4-mod-tweaks-<version>.zip` from [Releases](../../releases) and extract it.
3. Copy the extracted `mods` folder into the game's installation folder (the folder containing `WayOfTheSamurai4.exe`). If a `mods` folder already exists, merge the contents into it.
4. Confirm that `{game installation folder}/mods/tweaks/mod.dll` exists, then start the game.

## Settings

Switch the tweaks on the "Tweaks" page added to the options on the title screen and to 各種設定 in the pause menu. With more than 8 options, the page scrolls up and down.

Your settings are saved to `{game installation folder}/mods/tweaks/config.ini` when the game saves its options, and are kept the next time you start the game.
