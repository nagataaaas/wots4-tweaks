# tweaks

[日本語](README.md) | [Changelog](CHANGELOG.en.md)

> [!NOTE]
> This Mod is for the [Steam version of Way of the Samurai 4](https://store.steampowered.com/app/312780/4/?l=english) only.

A collection of small "nice to have" tweaks. Each tweak can be changed individually on the "Tweaks" page added to the game's options (便利機能 in the Japanese game).

## Features

- **Laura's Night Crawling**: choose who waits in the futon when you have a night crawling appointment with Laura.
  - Don't Fix: unchanged from the game. Who you get depends on things like whether you restarted the game after making the appointment.
  - Fake: when you lift the futon, it turns out to be someone else disguised as Laura.
  - Real: Laura herself is waiting.
- **Guaranteed Loot**: searching or kicking a body always gives an item on the first try, as long as the body has something to give. The odds of which item you get are unchanged. Items that townsfolk drop on their own when they die are not affected.
- **Unlimited My Style**: when creating a My Style, you can add as many moves as you like, even without enough Ability Points. The "total / Ability Points" display is unchanged, so the total may appear to exceed your points. No Ability Points are spent.
- **Mixed-Stance My Style**: when creating a My Style, a stance can also take moves of the other stances of the same weapon. The sword stances (Jo-dan, Chu-dan, Ge-dan, Waki, Iai, One-Handed and Shinobi) mix with each other, and so do the dual sword stances (Dual Arms and Shinobi Dual Arms). Iai moves taken into another stance do not sheathe the sword at their end. In addition, "Common" gathers the defensive moves of every stance, so that any weapon's stance can take them.
  - The move select screen gets a "Switch Stance" button. It opens a list of the stances and shows the moves of the one you pick. The moves chosen on each stance stay chosen when you switch.
  - "Common", at the end of the list of stances, holds the parries, Toughen, Speed-Eating, Eat Everything, Speed-Drinking, Drink Everything and Immovable Heart of every stance, and the stance's own Renki Koho. You can choose the moves you have learned in any stance. Other stances' Renki Koho is not listed, as it works only while you fight in its own stance.
  - The Spear, Hand-to-Hand, Pistol and Rifle stances can choose "Common" through "Switch Stance" too.
  - Combos link only moves of the same original stance.
  - The Ability Points total counts the moves chosen on every stance.
  - A first move's command and a defensive move's type can still be set only once, across stances too. First moves taken on another stance are dimmed, and choosing one tells you which stance has it, such as "(Jo-dan)".
  - Auto-Select and Copy Stance choose moves of the slot's own stance only.
  - Turning this option off again keeps the moves of the styles you made usable in battle.
- **All My Style Moves**: when creating a My Style, you can choose moves you have not learned yet just like the ones you have. Stances in which you have learned no moves can be chosen too, and Copy Stance copies every move. What you have learned stays as it is. A change applies from the next time you open the move list.
- **Instant Missions**: a mission succeeds as soon as you accept it and the conversation ends. You get the same rewards, town order and records as for a normal success, except for bonus rewards (such as those for the number of enemies defeated). Night missions accepted during the day also succeed right away.
  - This includes the mission chains of Nodobotoke, Kobato, the Thief, Gramps and Shirube, battle missions such as Underbelly Recon and Government Recon, and the final mission, The Amihama Eight. Clients offer their next request just as they would after a normal success.
  - The fights and conversations with the targets are skipped. Accepting Part 1 of Grandpa's Memories or A Lid On Something Stinky also accepts Part 2, and both parts succeed.
- **Guaranteed Wooing**: wooing always ends in a nighttime promise, no matter how you answer. Wooing a named character of Nightly Pleasure Martial Arts (Akagi, Kogure, Kotobuki, Moro, Kinugawa, J.J. or Dojima) also succeeds on the first try, whatever your level in the style. People who cannot be wooed still turn you down, as does everyone once you already have a partner for tonight.
- **Best Smithing**: when you ask the Smithy to Refine or Recast a weapon to make it stronger, its attack always rises by the maximum amount (Dojima's extra bonus is maxed too). Making a weapon harder or polishing it already gives fixed amounts, so those are unchanged.
- **Unlimited Refining**: the Smithy refines a weapon (to make it stronger or harder) even when its degradation has reached the weapon's maximum. From degradation 21, making it stronger raises attack by degradation + 1. The caps of 500 attack, 2000 maximum durability and 999 degradation stay.
- **Guaranteed Recruiting**: asking someone to become your pupil always works. You are still turned down if you have no dojo, if the dojo is closed, or if it is full (20 pupils).
- **Pupils Survive Challengers**: while challengers are at the dojo, talking to the pupil who brings the news leads to an ordinary conversation instead of a pupil dying or the dojo falling, so no pupil dies because of it. Pupils who fall in the fight at the dojo, or whom you cut down in town, still die as usual.
- **Pupils Stay in the Dojo**: your pupils no longer go out into town and stay in the dojo instead. When challengers come, no pupil comes into town to tell you. The number of pupils and the dojo's reputation are unaffected.
- **Repelling Challengers**: choose whether the sensei and your pupils repel dojo challengers (decided each time you move to another area).
  - Default: unchanged from the game.
  - Always: the sensei or a pupil always repels them.
  - Never: neither the sensei nor your pupils repel them.
- **Challenger Appearance**: choose whether dojo challengers come (decided each time you move to another area).
  - Default: unchanged from the game.
  - Always: challengers come whenever they can. As in the game, they never come at night, when you are leaving the dojo, when you have no pupils, or when the dojo's reputation is at its lowest.
  - Never: challengers never come.
- **Challenger Rep and ☆1 to ☆5 Challenger**: choose who the dojo challengers are (decided each time you enter the dojo). How often challengers come, and how often the sensei or your pupils repel them, stay the same.
  - Challenger Rep: challengers of the chosen reputation (☆1 to ☆5) come, whatever your dojo's reputation. With Don't Fix, they follow your dojo's reputation.
  - ☆1 to ☆5 Challenger: when challengers of that reputation come, the chosen ones come. With Don't Fix, the game picks among the challengers of that reputation.
  - In the game, the Half-naked Revolutionary, Foreign Nurse and Masked Bikini Women of ☆5 Challenger come at ☆5 only under certain conditions. When you choose one of them, they come regardless.
- **Enemy Spawn Interval**: choose how often the enemies that keep coming as you defeat them appear. This covers battle missions such as Underbelly Recon and the battles of the story.
  - Default: about one every second.
  - 0.5 s, 0.2 s, 0.1 s: one comes at that interval.
- **More Enemies at Once**: in battle missions, up to 8 of these enemies are out at once instead of 6. Story battles already bring out all the enemies they have, so they stay the same.
- **Enemy Pool Size**: in battle missions, the enemies that keep coming as you defeat them number 16 or 24 instead of 8, so new ones keep coming even while bodies lie in view. More Enemies at Once raises how many are out at once. Story battles stay the same. The setting takes effect from the next battle mission that starts.
- **Dropped Item Limit**: choose how many weapons and how many items can lie on the ground. Past the limit, the oldest disappear. Save the setting and restart the game for it to fully take effect (until then, weapons and items together stop at 60).
  - Default: 30 weapons and 30 items.
  - 60, 120: up to that many of each.
- **Defeated Enemy Cleanup**: no new enemy comes until a defeated one's body is cleared. Choose how bodies are cleared.
  - Default: a body is cleared about a second after it falls, once it is off the screen. An enemy still going down, as in a Spring Harvest, is not cleared until it has fallen.
  - Off Screen at Once: a body off the screen is cleared at once, even while it is still going down.
  - Even On Screen: about a second after an enemy starts going down, its body disappears even on the screen, along with anything you could have looted from it.
- **Weapon Durability**: choose how weapons lose durability. Guns are included.
  - Default: as in the game.
  - No Loss: the durability of your weapons does not go down when you strike or guard, and they do not break. Other characters' weapons wear down and break as before.
  - Break at Once: anyone's weapon, yours included, breaks the first time it would lose durability. Weapons that never lose durability in the game do not break.
- **Setup Anytime**: choosing Load on the title screen brings up the difficulty selection and the character creation even for a save from the middle of a playthrough. In the game, these screens come up only for a save made after a playthrough ends. Once you save the character, you carry on from where the save left off. Quitting the character creation takes you back to the title screen.
- **Full Spring Harvest Gauge**: the Spring Harvest gauge is always full and does not go down during a Spring Harvest. Enemies' Spring Harvests are not affected.
- **All Unlocks**: unlocks you have not bought on the Unlocks screen can be used as if bought: the Hard and Very Hard difficulties, the character's skin and hair colors, faces, hairstyles, clothes and body type change, the gun, rifle and dual sword licenses (equipping, drawing, buying at shops and the weapon kinds of My Style), and the chief minister's gun at the lost-and-found shop. The Unlocks screen and what you have bought stay as they are.
  - The weapon chests are not included, since buying them enlarges the chest. The accessories of unlocks can be chosen with All Accessories.
  - Which weapons enemies drop (no guns or dual swords without the licenses) is unchanged.
- **All Appearances**: the character's Appearance settings let you choose every set of clothes, hairstyle, face and character set, including ones you have not obtained. Appearances of downloadable content can be chosen only when you have that content. Appearances that come from unlocks can be chosen with All Unlocks. What the clothes shop and the hairdresser sell is unchanged.
- **All Accessories**: the accessory screen lets you choose every accessory, including ones you do not own. Accessories of downloadable content can be chosen only when you have that content. Shops, the records screen and gifts are unchanged.
- **Big Shot in Move List**: moves whose power the Big Shot weapon charm raises get a mark after "Str" on their cards in the Move List and when choosing My Style moves.
  - BS: the Big Shot bonus also grows with the weapon's attack power.
  - BS-: the Big Shot bonus is only added as it is and does not grow with the weapon's attack power (moves such as kicks and unarmed moves).
  - Big Shot does not work on moves without a mark. The setting applies from the next time you open a move list.

## Installation

### Automatic installation (recommended)

1. Close the game.
2. Download and run [WayOfTheSamurai4ModInstaller.exe](https://github.com/nagataaaas/wots4-mods/releases/latest/download/WayOfTheSamurai4ModInstaller.exe).
   - If "Windows protected your PC" appears, click "More info" → "Run anyway".
   - When asked whether to allow it to make changes as administrator, click "Yes" (it writes to the game folder).
3. Follow the screen and choose `tweaks` in the list of Mods. Move with Up and Down, choose with Space and confirm with Enter.

The ModLoader is installed along with it. The same steps update the Mod.

## Uninstallation

1. Close the game.
2. Delete the `{game installation folder}/mods/tweaks/` folder. The settings in it (`config.ini`) are deleted too.

## Settings

Change the tweaks on the "Tweaks" page (便利機能 in the Japanese game), which is added to the options on the title screen and to 各種設定 in the pause menu. The options are grouped under the headers Night Crawling, My Style, Smithy, Dojo and Other. Scroll the page up and down to see the options that do not fit. The options are shown in the game's language (Japanese or English).

Your settings are saved to `{game installation folder}/mods/tweaks/config.ini` when the game saves its options, and carry over the next time you start the game.
